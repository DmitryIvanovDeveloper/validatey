# 📚 User Stories: 5 SaaS Проектов 2026

**Формат:** As a [user role], I want [functionality] so that [benefit]  
**Методология:** Agile user story mapping для product development

---

## 📱 **Проект 1: AIVA - AI Personal Productivity Assistant**

### **🎯 Core User: Knowledge Worker (Office/Remote)**

#### **Task Management & Prioritization**
- **As a** busy project manager, **I want** AIVA to automatically prioritize my tasks based on deadlines and dependencies, **so that** I can focus on high-impact work instead of manual task sorting
- **As a** software developer, **I want** AIVA to suggest optimal coding sessions based on my energy patterns, **so that** I can maximize productivity during peak focus times
- **As a** marketing coordinator, **I want** AIVA to identify and flag urgent client emails requiring immediate response, **so that** I never miss critical communications

#### **Context Switching & Workflow**
- **As a** hybrid worker, **I want** AIVA to automatically save and restore my workspace context when switching between tasks, **so that** I minimize cognitive load during transitions
- **As a** consultant, **I want** AIVA to track time spent on different client projects automatically, **so that** I can accurately bill clients and optimize my work patterns
- **As a** team lead, **I want** AIVA to suggest meeting times that optimize attendance across different time zones, **so that** I can improve team collaboration efficiency

#### **Integration & Privacy**
- **As a** privacy-conscious employee, **I want** AIVA to process all my data locally on my device, **so that** my work patterns remain confidential from employers
- **As a** Microsoft Teams user, **I want** AIVA to integrate seamlessly with my Teams calendar and tasks, **so that** I don't need to maintain separate productivity tools
- **As a** Slack user, **I want** AIVA to monitor channel activity and suggest when to check important discussions, **so that** I stay connected without constant monitoring

## 🌱 **Проект 2: EcoTrack Pro - Corporate Sustainability Intelligence**

### **🎯 Core User: Sustainability Manager (Enterprise)**

#### **Emissions Tracking & Reporting**
- **As a** sustainability officer, **I want** EcoTrack to automatically calculate my company's carbon emissions from utility bills and travel data, **so that** I can generate accurate ESG reports without manual data entry
- **As a** CFO, **I want** EcoTrack to forecast future emissions costs under different climate scenarios, **so that** I can make informed investment decisions
- **As a** procurement manager, **I want** EcoTrack to score suppliers based on their carbon footprint, **so that** I can prioritize sustainable vendors

#### **Compliance & Regulatory**
- **As a** compliance officer, **I want** EcoTrack to automatically update ESG reporting templates when regulations change, **so that** my reports always meet current standards
- **As a** board member, **I want** EcoTrack to provide real-time sustainability KPIs on executive dashboards, **so that** I can monitor progress toward climate goals
- **As a** investor relations manager, **I want** EcoTrack to generate standardized sustainability disclosures for SEC filings, **so that** I can comply with emerging climate disclosure requirements

#### **Supplier & Supply Chain**
- **As a** supply chain director, **I want** EcoTrack to map my entire supplier network's carbon emissions, **so that** I can identify high-impact reduction opportunities
- **As a** sustainability consultant, **I want** EcoTrack to benchmark my client's emissions against industry peers, **so that** I can recommend targeted improvement strategies
- **As a** small business owner, **I want** EcoTrack to provide simple carbon tracking tools that integrate with my QuickBooks data, **so that** I can participate in sustainability programs affordably

## 🧠 **Проект 3: MindGuard Enterprise - Workplace Mental Wellness**

### **🎯 Core User: HR Manager (Enterprise)**

#### **Employee Monitoring & Early Detection**
- **As a** people operations manager, **I want** MindGuard to analyze employee communication patterns to detect early signs of burnout, **so that** I can provide timely support before productivity declines
- **As a** team leader, **I want** MindGuard to alert me when team members show stress indicators in meetings, **so that** I can have supportive conversations
- **As a** employee, **I want** MindGuard to provide anonymous mental health resources when I need them, **so that** I can access support without workplace stigma

#### **Wellness Programs & Analytics**
- **As a** wellness coordinator, **I want** MindGuard to design personalized wellness plans based on employee stress patterns, **so that** I can provide targeted preventive care
- **As a** C-suite executive, **I want** MindGuard to show company-wide mental health trends in leadership dashboards, **so that** I can make data-driven decisions about employee wellbeing investments
- **As a** benefits manager, **I want** MindGuard to integrate with my employee assistance program, **so that** members get seamless access to mental health services

#### **Privacy & Compliance**
- **As a** privacy officer, **I want** MindGuard to use end-to-end encryption for all employee data, **so that** mental health information remains confidential and HIPAA-compliant
- **As a** union representative, **I want** MindGuard to allow employees to opt-out of monitoring entirely, **so that** workers maintain control over their personal data
- **As a** diversity officer, **I want** MindGuard to provide mental health analytics broken down by demographic groups, **so that** I can identify and address inequities in wellbeing support

## 🎨 **Проект 4: CreatorFlow - Creator Economy Monetization Suite**

### **🎯 Core User: Content Creator (Mid-tier Influencer)**

#### **Revenue Aggregation & Analytics**
- **As a** YouTube creator, **I want** CreatorFlow to automatically track and combine revenue from YouTube ads, sponsorships, and merchandise, **so that** I can see my total earnings in one dashboard
- **As a** TikTok influencer, **I want** CreatorFlow to analyze which types of content generate the most engagement, **so that** I can optimize my content strategy for better monetization
- **As a** podcaster, **I want** CreatorFlow to manage sponsorship deals and track affiliate revenue from multiple platforms, **so that** I can focus on content creation rather than accounting

#### **Brand Partnerships & Contracts**
- **As a** lifestyle blogger, **I want** CreatorFlow to match me with relevant brand partnerships based on my audience demographics, **so that** I can find authentic collaboration opportunities
- **As a** beauty influencer, **I want** CreatorFlow to use smart contracts for brand deals with automatic revenue sharing, **so that** I trust the payment terms are fair and transparent
- **As a** fitness coach, **I want** CreatorFlow to negotiate better rates for sponsored content based on my engagement metrics, **so that** I maximize earnings from brand partnerships

#### **Multi-Platform Management**
- **As a** multi-platform creator, **I want** CreatorFlow to schedule and cross-post content across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube simultaneously, **so that** I can maintain presence on all platforms efficiently
- **As a** content creator, **I want** CreatorFlow to provide real-time analytics on which platforms drive the most revenue, **so that** I can allocate my time effectively
- **As a** creator agency manager, **I want** CreatorFlow to manage multiple creators' revenue streams and generate consolidated reports, **so that** I can provide transparent accounting to my clients

## 🌍 **Проект 5: ClimateGuard - Enterprise Climate Risk Assessment**

### **🎯 Core User: Risk Manager (Enterprise)**

#### **Risk Assessment & Modeling**
- **As a** chief risk officer, **I want** ClimateGuard to model how extreme weather events could impact my company's facilities and operations, **so that** I can develop comprehensive business continuity plans
- **As a** CFO, **I want** ClimateGuard to quantify the financial impact of climate change on my company's assets and supply chain, **so that** I can make informed investment decisions
- **As a** facilities manager, **I want** ClimateGuard to predict when my buildings might be at risk from flooding or heat waves, **so that** I can implement preventive maintenance

#### **Regulatory Compliance & Reporting**
- **As a** compliance officer, **I want** ClimateGuard to automatically generate climate risk disclosures for SEC filings, **so that** I meet new regulatory requirements efficiently
- **As a** sustainability director, **I want** ClimateGuard to track changes in climate regulations across jurisdictions, **so that** I can ensure ongoing compliance
- **As a** investor relations manager, **I want** ClimateGuard to provide standardized climate risk data for ESG ratings, **so that** I can communicate transparently with stakeholders

#### **Scenario Planning & Strategy**
- **As a** strategy director, **I want** ClimateGuard to simulate different climate scenarios and their impact on my business model, **so that** I can develop resilient long-term strategies
- **As a** supply chain director, **I want** ClimateGuard to assess climate risks in my supplier network, **so that** I can diversify sourcing and reduce vulnerability
- **As a** insurance manager, **I want** ClimateGuard to optimize my company's insurance coverage based on climate risk profiles, **so that** I can balance cost and protection effectively

## 🎯 **Acceptance Criteria Templates**

### **📝 Functional Requirements**
```
Given: [initial context]
When: [user action]
Then: [expected outcome]
And: [additional verification]
```

### **🔒 Non-Functional Requirements**
```
Performance: Response time < 2 seconds for 95% of requests
Security: End-to-end encryption for all user data
Scalability: Support 10,000+ concurrent users
Compliance: Meet industry-specific regulations
Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
```

### **🧪 Testing Scenarios**
```
Happy Path: User completes task successfully
Edge Cases: Invalid data, network failures, permission issues
Error Handling: Graceful degradation, clear error messages
Performance: Load testing under various conditions
Security: Penetration testing, data protection validation
```

## 🚀 **Implementation Notes**

### **📱 Mobile-First Considerations**
- **AIVA:** Native mobile apps for productivity tracking on-the-go
- **EcoTrack:** Mobile access for field sustainability assessments
- **MindGuard:** Mobile wellness check-ins and anonymous support access
- **CreatorFlow:** Mobile content scheduling and analytics
- **ClimateGuard:** Mobile alerts for climate-related risks

### **🔗 Integration Priorities**
- **AIVA:** Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Teams
- **EcoTrack:** SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, utility APIs
- **MindGuard:** HRIS systems, healthcare providers, insurance platforms
- **CreatorFlow:** All major social platforms, payment processors, tax services
- **ClimateGuard:** Climate data APIs, financial systems, regulatory databases

### **🎨 UI/UX Principles**
- **Clean, professional interfaces** for enterprise users
- **Intuitive workflows** that reduce cognitive load
- **Progressive disclosure** of advanced features
- **Accessibility-first design** for all user types
- **Mobile-responsive** for hybrid work scenarios
